Divina Argila: 4 systems integrated in 40 days

Divina Argila had fragmented processes and tasks repeated in several places. We integrated Bling, Trello, TikTok Shop and Mercado Livre: every e-commerce order lands in Trello, the factory team creates and ships from a single place, and sellers go back to selling.

The case in questions

How the project went.

Website of Divina Argila →

Who is Divina Argila?

Divina Argila is a company with its own factory that sells through e-commerce channels such as Mercado Livre and TikTok Shop. It is an Agência Byte client.

What was the problem?

Processes were fragmented and several were done repeatedly. Sellers lost time fixing the same order in 4 different places instead of selling on the channels.

What did Agência Byte do?

It integrated Bling, Trello, TikTok Shop and Mercado Livre. Everything that happens on the e-commerce platforms goes automatically into Trello, so the factory team can create and ship orders.

What were the numbers?

4 systems integrated in 40 days, and order tracking went from 4 different places to just 1, Trello.

What changed for the team?

The factory team now looks only at Trello to create and ship. Sellers no longer fix orders across several systems and can focus on selling on the channels.

How long did it take?

40 days.

Which systems were integrated?

Bling, Trello, TikTok Shop and Mercado Livre.
