In practice

What changes in each area.

Sales and customer service

before

Leads come in via WhatsApp, forms and email, and someone copies everything into the CRM. After business hours, nobody replies.

after

An AI agent answers 24/7, qualifies the contact and logs everything in the CRM. Your team gets leads already triaged.

Artificial Intelligence Automation →

Orders and inventory

before

Online store orders are re-typed into the ERP. Website inventory doesn't match reality, and selling out-of-stock items turns into rework.

after

Store, ERP and inventory synced automatically. An order is entered once and flows through every system on its own.

Systems Integration →

Internal processes

before

Approvals by email, controls in shared spreadsheets and no visibility into who owns each task.

after

A custom system with approval workflows, permissions and history, designed around your process.

Custom Software Development →

Management and metrics

before

Reports built by hand at month-end from exports. Each department reports a different number.

after

Power BI dashboards updated automatically, with a single definition for every metric.
