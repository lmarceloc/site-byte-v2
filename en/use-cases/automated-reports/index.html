Use case

Reports that build themselves

Data comes straight from your systems, numbers are always calculated the same way and the AI writes a summary of what changed. Every Monday, the report arrives ready.

Start a project → Chat on WhatsApp
The scenario

How it works today, without automation.

Every Friday, someone spends the afternoon exporting spreadsheets from the ERP, the CRM and finance, merging everything, building charts and writing the weekly summary. When that person goes on vacation, there is no report.

  1. 01Hours every week spent merging spreadsheets instead of analyzing.
  2. 02Each department calculates the same metric differently.
  3. 03A formula error goes unnoticed and decisions are made on the wrong number.
  4. 04The report arrives late, when the problem has already grown.
How it works with AI

The flow, step by step.

  1. 01

    Data comes from your systems

    ERP, CRM, finance and spreadsheets are read automatically.

  2. 02

    Metrics calculated

    Each number follows a single definition, the same for every department.

  3. 03

    AI writes the summary

    What went up, what went down and what needs attention, in plain language.

  4. 04

    Report delivered

    Arrives by email, WhatsApp or Teams on the agreed day and time.

  5. 05

    Details on the dashboard

    Anyone who wants to dig deeper opens the dashboard with the same numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about this use case.

Can the AI get the numbers wrong?

The numbers aren't calculated by the AI: they come straight from the systems, with fixed formulas. The AI only writes the text from those numbers.

Can I get a daily report as well as the weekly one?

Yes. You set the frequency and delivery time, and each person can receive only what matters to their department.

Do I need Power BI?

No. The report can arrive by email or WhatsApp only. The Power BI dashboard is optional, for those who want the details.

What if a system has no API?

We can read automatic exports, the database or shared spreadsheets. We assess the best route during discovery.

Want to see how it would work in your company?

You explain the problem. We build the solution. A straight answer, no generic proposal.
