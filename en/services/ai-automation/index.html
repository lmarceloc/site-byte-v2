Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

Will the AI make decisions on its own?

Only where you decide. Sensitive steps go through human approval, and every AI decision is logged for auditing.

Is my data safe?

Data only flows between your systems and the providers chosen for the project, with restricted access and usage logs.

How much does it cost to run an AI automation?

The running cost is estimated before we start, based on task volume, and monitored after launch so there are no surprises on the bill.
