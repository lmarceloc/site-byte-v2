Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

Do I need a Power BI license?

It depends on how the dashboards will be shared. During discovery we recommend the right license for your number of users and usage.

Where does the data come from?

From the systems you already use: ERP, CRM, e-commerce, spreadsheets and APIs. The pipelines update everything automatically.

What if the source data is messy?

That's the most common scenario. Part of the work is cleaning, standardizing and validating the data before it reaches the dashboard.
