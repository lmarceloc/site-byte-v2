When it makes sense

Signs it's time.

  1. 01Your process runs on shared spreadsheets nobody trusts anymore.
  2. 02The off-the-shelf system needs workarounds to fit your operation.

What we deliver

Service scope.

Internal systems

Records, approval workflows, order, inventory and operations control, with role-based permissions.

Portals and member areas

Portals for customers, resellers or suppliers, with authentication, history and self-service.

APIs and back end

Documented APIs to connect your system to apps, partners and other platforms.

Management dashboards

Operational screens with the numbers your team needs every day, without exporting spreadsheets.

How we work

5 steps, from diagnosis to support.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We map the current process, the systems involved and where the bottleneck is.

    2 to 3 days
  2. 02

    Strategy

    Technical proposal, stack, timeline and investment defined in writing.

    2 to 4 days
  3. 03

    Development

    Short sprints with partial deliveries in a staging environment.

    3 to 5 weeks
  4. 04

    Validation

    Testing, final adjustments and sign-off. QA, security and performance.

    1 week
  5. 05

    Support

    Deploy, documentation, training and continuous evolution.

    ongoing
Frequently asked questions

Common questions.

How long does a project take?

A first module in production usually takes 3 to 6 weeks. Larger projects are split into smaller deliveries, so you can use the system before the project ends.

Does my company own the code?

Yes. The code is written and maintained by our own team, with no outsourcing, and it lives in your company's repository.

Do you provide maintenance after delivery?

Yes. We offer support with an SLA of up to 4 business hours and keep evolving the system with you after go-live.
