# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Decision-makers at startups, PMEs, and mid-market companies in Brazil (and abroad) who have a real engineering problem and not enough time to solve it themselves. CTOs, founders, and operations leads who evaluate agencies on technical credibility — they can tell the difference between real engineering and marketing speak. They arrive with a specific problem (slow onboarding, manual process, fragile integration) and need to trust that Agencia Byte understands it before they'll fill out a form.

## Product Purpose

Agencia Byte is a Brazilian engineering agency specializing in custom software, AI automation, and system integrations. The site's job is to convert qualified visitors into project briefs — not by selling a product, but by demonstrating technical competence and making the agency's process tangible. Success means a decision-maker reading three paragraphs and thinking "these people get it."

## Brand Personality

Precisa · Profissional · Séria. Engineering-grade confidence, not startup bravado. The site speaks like the senior engineer who knows the answer before you finish the question — no fluff, no hype, no vague promises. Results and process are foregrounded; aesthetics serve credibility, not the other way around.

## Anti-references

- **SaaS genérico**: Purple gradients, hero metric grids, "10x your growth" copy. The visual shorthand of generic SaaS is exactly wrong here — it reads as tech marketing, not engineering.
- **Outsourcing barato**: Dense walls of text, stock photography, no distinctive voice. Cheap offshore dev shops over-explain and under-demonstrate.
- **Agências criativas**: Image-heavy portfolios where the site is the product. Agencia Byte builds systems, not aesthetics.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't explain**: Demonstrate capability through live UI (terminal animation, pipeline demo) rather than describing it in copy.
2. **Credibilidade técnica primeiro**: Every visual decision should read as engineering rigor — typography, mono details, grid precision — not marketing polish.
3. **Substância antes de estética**: Content and structure lead; decoration only when it earns its place.
4. **Confiança sem distância**: Serious and precise, but not cold. The tone is a senior engineer who is also a clear communicator.
5. **Presença local, alcance global**: PT-BR primary. Design must work for Brazilian business culture — directness, relationship-first — while projecting international-grade quality (BR · US · ES).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AAA target. Minimum contrast ratios of 7:1 for normal text, 4.5:1 for large text. All interactive elements keyboard-accessible with visible focus states. No timing-dependent content that can't be paused. No reliance on color alone to convey meaning. Reduced motion support via `prefers-reduced-motion`.
